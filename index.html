About me

Junior 3D Artist focused on 3D art and atmospheric scenes

I am a beginner 3D Artist with a focus on hard-surface modeling, 3D art and atmospheric environments. I work with Blender, Substance 3D Painter and Marvelous Designer: modeling, UVs, baking, texturing and learning the 3D pipeline.

I am currently building my portfolio and developing a personal project: a classic point-and-click adventure. I create mechanics, logic, locations, interactive objects and the overall visual style of the world.
