About me

Looking for work, freelance tasks or an internship

I am a beginner 3D Artist with a focus on hard-surface modeling, 3D art and atmospheric environments. I work with Blender, Substance 3D Painter and Marvelous Designer: modeling, UVs, baking, texturing and learning the game asset pipeline.

I am currently building my portfolio and developing a personal project: a classic point-and-click adventure. I create mechanics, logic, locations, interactive objects and the overall visual style of the world.

  • I am especially interested in game development, but I am open to different 3D-related tasks.
  • Looking for an internship, junior position or project-based work.
